# AX-1177 (anti-IL-33 mAb): portfolio audit position

**Scope.** This review covers all five delivered files: the methods annex, mmc2 (serum proteomics), mmc3 (post-mortem lung IHC), mmc4 (HUVEC IL-33 vs vehicle) and mmc5 (the other 24 contrasts). Every number below comes from those files. Where I use general knowledge from outside our data, I say so. *Compound_47 = AX-1177; NIP228 = isotype control.*

---

## 1. Recommendation

**KEEP AX-1177, but retire "viral LRTD" as its development indication. Re-scope it to a narrower, biomarker-gated hypothesis and fund only two short gates with pre-set kill criteria. If Gate 1 fails, drop it.**

The re-scoped hypothesis is that IL-33 drives activation of the vascular endothelium in hospitalised pneumonia, in sST2-high patients.

**Why keep.**
- Our data show that, in vitro, AX-1177 does what it was built to do.
- The only IL-33-specific protein we measured in patients (sST2) is the #2 predictor of progression out of 1,063 proteins.
- The decisive missing experiments can be run mostly on material already in our archive: count matrices, the 100-patient serum bank and lung-tissue RNA from 22 donors.

**Why not as "viral LRTD".** Our data never test the viral-LRTD hypothesis.
- There is no infection and no airway-epithelial IL-33 pharmacology.
- There is no IL-33 measurement in patients.
- There is no IL-33 change in diseased lung.
- The patient severity signal does not carry AX-1177's in vitro footprint.

Continuing the programme as framed would mean investing on assumption, not evidence.

## 3. The case for keeping

### 3.1 Pharmacology is real, large and clean (Fig. 1)
- **IL-33red drives a large endothelial inflammatory programme.**
  - In HUVEC, 30 ng/mL IL-33red changes **4,031 genes at 6 h** and **1,836 at 24 h** (FDR < 0.05).
  - The dominant Hallmark pathways are TNF/NF-κB, inflammatory response, IL-6/JAK/STAT3 and interferon (NES 2.6–3.1).
- **AX-1177 neutralises it, under conservative conditions.**
  - Dose: 10 µg/mL, roughly a 41-fold molar excess. It was added at the same time as IL-33, with no pre-incubation.
  - I measured reversal using independent arms: (AX-1177+IL-33 vs isotype+IL-33) against (IL-33 vs vehicle). This avoids regression-to-the-mean bias.
  - **6 h:** median **95%** reversal across 639 induced genes. For the 131 genes induced >8-fold, **97%** reversal, and 85% are individually significant.
  - **24 h:** median 73%, and 82% for genes induced >8-fold.
- **The effect generalises across endothelial beds.**
  - In glomerular microvascular endothelium (HGMEC) at 6 h, AX-1177 reverses the same core programme.
  - That programme is 133 genes, including **GM-CSF, G-CSF, CXCL2/3/5/6, CCL20, CX3CL1, IL-6, TNF, IL-1α/β, OPG and C3**. Its function is neutrophil recruitment, myelopoiesis and endothelial activation.
- **No intrinsic activity.**
  - Once low-count genes, trivial-effect artefacts and SE-flagged genes are removed, 0–12 antibody-alone hits remain per contrast. None is inflammatory.
  - The 133-gene core programme does not move with antibody alone (no agonism).

### 3.2 The IL-33 axis carries prognostic information in patients (Fig. 2)
- **sST2 (soluble IL-33 receptor / decoy) is the #2 protein of 1,063** for ICU, invasive ventilation or death within 30 days.
  - Cohort: 100 hospitalised COVID-19 patients, 44 events.
  - Adjusted for age, sex and NEWS2, so the signal is additional to bedside severity.
  - It is not a cardiac-strain proxy: NT-proBNP is weak (OR 1.24, q = 0.15).
- **Products that AX-1177 suppresses in vitro are also prognostic:**
  - IL-6: q = 0.012, concordant on 4 independent panels.
  - OPG: q = 0.014.
  - CCL20: q = 0.020.
  - IL-1β: q = 0.046.
  - CSF-1: q = 0.037.

### 3.3 The IL-33 reservoir sits where the drug acts (Fig. 3)
- **IL-33 protein is abundant in lung endothelium and basal epithelium in all 31 lungs.**
  - Endothelial median score 4.8–5.5, against a recorded maximum of 5.5.
- **Exploratory signal consistent with IL-33 release during inflammation:**
  - Lower endothelial IL-33 where neutrophil elastase is higher (donor-level ρ = −0.43, p = 0.016; same after age adjustment).
  - Lower endothelial IL-33 in lungs with acute pathology on autopsy (p = 0.028).
- Together these fit an endothelial IL-33 loop: IL-33 released from endothelium acts back on endothelium, which is exactly what AX-1177 blocks in vitro.

## 4. Anticipated challenges, and the honest answers

1. **"You never measured IL-33 in patients."**
   - Correct. The Olink IL33 assay is absent from the delivery, along with IL-4, IL-5, IL-13 and TSLP, which suggests a detectability filter.
   - Pathway activity is therefore inferred from sST2 alone. *This is what Gate 1b fixes, using archived serum.*
2. **"sST2 is a generic severity marker."**
   - Largely true. The severity signal is very broad: 169 of 1,063 proteins reach FDR < 0.05, and 82% of all ORs are > 1.
   - Intracellular-leak proteins (NPM1, PXN, CALR) also rank near the top.
   - sST2 sits 2.2 SD above this shifted background, but with summary statistics we cannot adjust it for IL-6/CRP or renal function.
   - sST2 is also a decoy: high sST2 could mean *less* free IL-33.
3. **"Where is the drug's footprint in patients?"**
   - This is the central weakness.
   - Proteins induced >16-fold by IL-33 in endothelium are *less* associated with outcome than background (median z 1.17 vs 1.36).
   - A weak overall positive gradient exists (ρ = 0.14). It is driven by weakly changed, largely intracellular proteins, i.e. cell injury, not IL-33 signalling.
4. **"Is IL-33 up in COVID lungs?"**
   - No, and this holds whether donors are grouped by the delivered labels or by the autopsy text.
5. **"Is this the usual type-2 IL-33 story?"**
   - No. Serum IL-5, eotaxins, tryptase and TARC are null.
   - The mechanism in our data is endothelial and neutrophil-centred.
6. **"Does the package test viral LRTD at all?"**
   - No:
     - no virus;
     - no airway epithelium exposed to IL-33 (A549/MCF7 got antibody only, and MCF7 is a breast line);
     - no lung microvascular cells;
     - no oxidised IL-33, even though dual binding is the claimed differentiator;
     - one dose, single donors, and no in vivo data.
7. **"Can we trust the data?"**
   - Mostly, but with defects (section 9). The worst is invalid statistics for 256 genes, including sST2/IL1RL1, PTX3, E-selectin, VCAM-1, ICAM-1 and IL-8.
   - This blocks those genes as in vitro bridging markers until re-analysed.
   - It does **not** affect the pharmacology conclusion, which rests on the 25,954 genes with valid statistics.

## 5. What indication our data actually point at

**They point at IL-33-driven activation of the vascular endothelium in severe, hospitalised pneumonia.** That is a non-type-2, neutrophil- and endothelium-centred mechanism in the population at risk of ICU admission, ventilation or death, enriched by high sST2 at admission.

**Working indication:** adults hospitalised with viral pneumonia who are sST2-high at enrolment, with prevention of progression (ICU/IMV/death) as the endpoint. Requiring supplemental oxygen is a proposed operational definition of "hospitalised"; our cohort data do not specify oxygen status.

This is **narrower** than viral LRTD and **mechanistically different** from the epithelial/type-2 rationale that usually underpins IL-33 programmes. Our data say nothing about:
- outpatient or mild disease;
- children;
- RSV or influenza specifically.

Two qualifiers:
- **The human data are pre-vaccination, single-centre SARS-CoV-2.** They must be replicated in contemporary influenza, RSV and SARS-CoV-2 pneumonia before any claim generalises.
- **The tissue data do not show the biology is virus-specific.** COVID-negative bronchopneumonia looks the same. But do not extend to bacterial pneumonia yet. AX-1177 abolishes endothelial G-CSF, GM-CSF and CXC-chemokine induction, which is the neutrophil host-defence programme, so bacterial pneumonia carries a mechanistic infection risk.

### Alternatives considered and ruled out
| Candidate | Why ruled out on our data |
|---|---|
| Broad viral LRTD (outpatient, paediatric, RSV) | No data in any of these populations. |
| Type-2 asthma / eosinophilic COPD | Type-2 markers null; no epithelial pharmacology. |
| COPD in general (where the external IL-33 field has gone) | Nothing in our data addresses it. Tissue IL-33 does not differ by COPD status (n = 11 vs 20). It would be a strategic pivot, not a data-led one. |
| Kidney / glomerular disease | HGMEC response is about half the size of HUVEC's (median reversal −0.78 vs −1.49 log2) and largely gone by 24 h (8% vs 42% of genes reversed). No kidney clinical data; KIM-1 q = 0.11. |
| Heart failure / cardiovascular (sST2 is an HF marker) | Our sST2 signal is not cardiac strain (NT-proBNP weak). No CV data. The IL-33/ST2 axis is generally described as cardioprotective (external knowledge), a safety concern. |
| Sepsis / bacterial pneumonia | Host-defence risk (above); no data. |

## 6. If we drop it: what we are dropping and why

**What we would be dropping:**
- A clean neutraliser of reduced IL-33 with demonstrated endothelial pharmacology (two vascular beds, ~95% reversal, no agonism). It works at ~41:1 molar excess; potency was never measured.
- One human prognostic link (sST2), not specific.

**What we would not be dropping:** a validated disease hypothesis. Our data contain none for viral LRTD.
- IL-33 is not measured in patients.
- IL-33 is not changed in diseased lung.
- There is no type-2 involvement.
- There is no airway or infection pharmacology.
- There is no in vivo efficacy.

**Why drop, if we do:** the disease linkage is unproven, and the one bridging test available (does the patient severity signal look like AX-1177's target biology?) is negative for the drug's most characteristic outputs.

## 7. What the next studies must establish (gated plan)

### Gate 1: archive-based, low cost (~3 months; estimate)
- **1a. Re-analyse the archived counts.**
   - Fit per cell line and confirm the core programme and its reversal.
   - Obtain valid statistics for E-selectin, VCAM-1, ICAM-1, IL-8, CCL2, PTX3, IL1RL1 and vWF.
- **1b. Same 100-patient serum bank.**
   - Measure free and total IL-33 and IL-33:sST2 complexes with a high-sensitivity assay, plus GM-CSF.
   - Re-measure sST2 on a clinical-grade assay.
   - With patient-level data, test three things:
     - (i) IL-33 readouts against the composite endpoint, adjusted for age, sex and NEWS2;
     - (ii) sST2 adjusted for IL-6/CRP and renal function;
     - (iii) correlation of sST2/IL-33 with the drug-reversible panel (CCL20, IL-6, OPG).
- **1c. Archived lung RNA (22 donors).**
   - Score the 133-gene core programme and relate it to neutrophil elastase and endothelial IL-33.

**GO to Gate 2 if** an IL-33-specific serum readout (free or total IL-33, or IL-33:sST2 complex) is quantifiable in most patients, is prognostic independent of NEWS2 and IL-6, and tracks CCL20/IL-6/OPG. The tissue-RNA result is supportive, not sufficient on its own: an in-situ NF-κB programme could be driven by TNF or IL-1.

**KILL if** IL-33 readouts are undetectable or unrelated to outcome **and** sST2's association disappears after adjusting for IL-6/CRP, whatever the tissue result.

### Gate 2: disease-relevant pharmacology (~6 months; estimate)
- **Cells:** primary lung microvascular endothelium and differentiated airway epithelium (ALI), at least 3 donors each.
- **Stimulus:** SARS-CoV-2, influenza or RSV infection, or conditioned medium from infected epithelium.
- **IL-33 forms:** both reduced and oxidised.
- **Dosing:** AX-1177 titration (IC50/IC90 against projected exposure), including delayed (therapeutic) addition.
- **Design:** readouts at 6 h and 24 h; vehicle in every line; randomised plate layout.

**GO if** AX-1177 reduces infection-driven (endogenous IL-33) endothelial activation by ≥50% at attainable concentrations. **KILL if** infection-driven activation is IL-33-independent.

### Gate 3: first-in-patient, biomarker-led study
- **Population:** hospitalised viral pneumonia, sST2-enriched.
- **Anchoring:** target engagement plus the PD panel.
- **Safety:** infection surveillance.

## 8. Biomarkers, sampling timepoint, and which go into patients
*(full evidence in `tables/T1_biomarker_proposal.csv`)*

| Marker | Role | Into patients? |
|---|---|---|
| **sST2** | Enrichment / prognostic (candidate predictive) | **Yes.** Serum at enrolment, pre-dose. Clinical-grade assays exist; confirm no drug interference. |
| **IL-33: total, free, IL-33:drug and IL-33:sST2 complexes** | Target engagement and pathway activity | **Yes, once assays are built.** This is the decisive missing measurement. |
| **CCL20** (lead), **IL-6**, **OPG** (+ IL-1β, CSF-1) | Pharmacodynamic / proof of mechanism (exploratory) | **Yes.** Serum at enrolment and ~24 h post-dose, plus Day 3/7. They are the only markers that are IL-33-induced, AX-1177-reversed *and* prognostic. |
| **GM-CSF**, G-CSF, CXCL6, CX3CL1 | Ex vivo functional bioassay readouts (patient serum + IL-33red on endothelial cells, read at **6 h**). GM-CSF also as a serum marker if detectable. | **Ex vivo only.** Robust in vitro, but not prognostic in serum (GM-CSF not measured). |
| HGF, PTX3, vWF, ANGPT2, sE-selectin | Prognostic covariates, not PD | Yes, as covariates |
| Procalcitonin, neutrophil count, infection surveillance | Safety | **Yes** (mechanism-driven infection risk) |
| Blood eosinophils, IL-5, eotaxins, tryptase; lung IL-33 IHC | Not recommended | No |
| E-selectin, VCAM-1, ICAM-1, IL-8 as PD | On hold | Not until the transcriptomics is re-analysed |

**Sampling timepoint.**
- **Clinical, for selection:** **enrolment (pre-dose)**. This is the only timepoint at which our data show prognostic value. Keep serum as the matrix, because that is what our associations were measured in.
- **Clinical, for PD:** sample early, at about **24 h after dosing**.
  - The core markers are primary NF-κB response genes whose IL-33-driven component decays quickly in vitro: IL-6 falls from 5.8 to 2.8 log2 between 6 h and 24 h, CX3CL1 from 8.4 to 3.2, and CCL20 from 9.2 to 5.6.
  - Record dexamethasone timing, which suppresses these markers.
- **In vitro and ex vivo: 6 h.**
  - The drug-reversible window is widest at 6 h: 4,239 vs 1,160 reversed genes in HUVEC, and 1,460 vs 269 in HGMEC.
  - Cross-bed concordance collapses by 24 h: 975 genes reversed in both beds at 6 h, 49 at 24 h.

## 9. Data-integrity findings the committee should know about
*(full log in `tables/T7_data_integrity_log.csv`)*

1. **mmc5 sheet names "6h"/"24h" are not timepoints.**
   - Both sheets hold all 24 contrasts with identical values.
   - "6h" is padded with 419,535 empty rows to Excel's row limit. Nothing is missing.
2. **256 genes have implausible standard errors**, identical across all contrasts. Examples: IL1RL1, PTX3, SELE, VCAM1, ICAM1, CXCL8, CXCL1, CCL2, VWF, CDH5.
   - This is consistent with variance pooled across four heterogeneous cell lines in the single 114-library model.
   - mmc4 is sorted by logFC, so several of these non-significant genes sit at the top (E-selectin, VCAM-1, IL-8, CXCL1).
3. **Design gaps:**
   - no HGMEC vehicle, so the IL-33 effect is not estimable in HGMEC;
   - epithelial lines were never given IL-33;
   - single concentration, single donors, reduced IL-33 only.
4. **mmc2 documentation problems:**
   - The BH adjustment implies about 1,194 tests, but 1,094 assays were delivered; 102 standard-panel assays are absent, including IL33.
   - Twenty-seven proteins appear on several panels. They are concordant, which is useful as technical replication, but they inflate hit counts: 178 significant assays are 169 unique proteins.
   - ORs are "per log unit" of an already-log2 readout, so they are not comparable across proteins.
   - Only summary statistics are provided.
5. **mmc3 problems:**
   - Donor-level fields sit only on the first block. Block-level analysis is pseudo-replicated: IL-5Rα gives p = 0.025 per block vs 0.49 per donor.
   - One Excel formula is stored as text.
   - Scores reach 5.5 on a 0–5 scale, and endothelial IL-33 is at ceiling in 53% of blocks.
   - **9 of 31 donors have disease labels that contradict their own autopsy text.** The IL-33 null result is robust to re-classification; the eosinophil findings are not.

## 10. What else I considered and ruled out (analytical)

- **Citing E-selectin, VCAM-1, IL-8 or CXCL1 as lead IL-33 genes.** Their large logFCs have invalid statistics.
- **Reading the antibody-alone hits as off-target pharmacology.** They are low-count noise, trivial effects with inflated t (e.g. AKR1C2 logFC 0.06, t = 77), or SE-flagged genes.
  - The antibody-alone pathway shifts go in opposite directions in HUVEC and HGMEC, which suggests technical variation.
- **The apparent isotype effect on IL-33-repressed genes.** It reverses sign depending on which arm is used for selection, so it is regression to the mean.
- **"No effect in epithelium".** Untestable: no IL-33 arm.
- **Ranking serum proteins by OR.** Scale-dependent (CSF-1 OR 90, CI 4–1,933). I ranked by z instead.
- **Counting 178 serum hits.** That is 169 unique proteins inside a global upward shift, so the count is not evidence of specificity.
- **Block-level tissue statistics.** Pseudo-replication.
- **Taking tissue group labels at face value.** I ran a pathology-text sensitivity analysis.
- **Claiming IL-33 potency or oxidised-form activity.** Not in the data.
