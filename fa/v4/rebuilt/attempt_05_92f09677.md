# AX-1177 (anti-IL-33 mAb): portfolio audit, evidence review and recommendation

**Scope.** This review covers the five files delivered for AX-1177: the methods annex, two RNA-seq contrast workbooks, the serum proteomics workbook and the post-mortem lung workbook. Every number below was computed from those files. Where I rely on general biology to interpret a result, it is marked **[background]**. Figures are in `figures/`, tables in `tables/`, and the code that regenerates all of them is in `scripts/`.

---

## 1. Decision summary

> **Recommendation: No. Do not keep AX-1177 as an active asset. Stop the severe lower respiratory viral infection programme now.**
> Before the asset is formally closed, take up to one quarter to recover three datasets that we already generated but that were not delivered. This needs no new wet-lab work and no new samples. Termination is the default outcome. The asset re-enters only if those data meet the pre-specified criteria in §4.5.

**The case in four points**

1. **The molecule works in a dish.**
   - AX-1177 removes **94–100 %** of the IL-33-driven endothelial activation programme at 6 h and **76–97 %** at 24 h (HUVEC).
   - It also neutralises the response in glomerular microvascular endothelium (HGMEC).
   - On its own it has no coherent transcriptional effect in four cell types. (Fig. 1, Fig. 3)
2. **Nothing in our human data links IL-33 to severe viral lung disease.**
   - *Lung tissue (31 donors):* IL-33 is not higher in COVID-19 lungs in any compartment. Where it differs, it is *lower*, and this statistically fragile difference follows acute, neutrophil-rich pneumonia of any cause rather than SARS-CoV-2. (Fig. 5)
   - *Patient serum (100 patients):* the proteins AX-1177 suppresses in vitro are no more prognostic than any other protein (22 % vs 17 % FDR-significant; Fisher p = 0.23). The size of the in-vitro IL-33 effect is uncorrelated with prognostic strength (Spearman ρ = 0.01, p = 0.95). (Fig. 4)
   - *sST2:* this is the one pathway-proximal signal (#2 of 1,094 assays). But it is the IL-33 decoy receptor **[background]**, and the other IL-1-family receptors and antagonists are prognostic in the same way (IL-1R2, IL-18R1, IL-1Ra; module p = 0.001). It cannot tell us whether IL-33 is active.
   - *IL-33 itself:* the one measurement that could settle the question appears to have been run but was not delivered. The FDR correction was computed over 1,194 assays, but the file holds 1,094, and IL-33 is not among them.
3. **The indication label is broader than the evidence.**
   - All human data come from one single-centre, pre-vaccination COVID-19 cohort and an elderly, all-white autopsy series.
   - Nothing covers influenza, RSV or other respiratory viruses.
4. **Several encouraging numbers in the deliverables do not survive checking.** An auditor would find them:
   - tissue p-values computed per histology block instead of per donor;
   - RNA-seq p-values that are invalid for 230 genes;
   - serum proteins counted two to four times across panels.

**What we would be dropping.** A clean, specific IL-33 neutraliser with well-characterised endothelial pharmacology, but with no human evidence that IL-33 drives the disease we are targeting.

**What our data point at.** At most, pathogen-agnostic severe pneumonia or acute lung injury with endothelial activation. That is a hypothesis to test (§4.2), not an indication to fund. Our data do **not** point to type-2/eosinophilic airway disease, COPD or kidney disease (§5).

### 2.2 Challenges we expect and our answers

- **"The pharmacology is excellent. Why drop it?"** The pharmacology is necessary, not sufficient.
  - It shows that AX-1177 binds and blocks reduced IL-33 at a ~41-fold molar excess (10 µg/mL ≈ 68 nM antibody vs 30 ng/mL ≈ 1.7 nM IL-33), added at the same time as the IL-33, in single-donor, non-lung endothelium.
  - It does not establish potency (there is no dose–response), activity against oxidised IL-33 (the annex's differentiating claim, untested), or activity in lung endothelium or epithelium.
  - The outputs it blocks are generic NF-κB mediators (IL-6, IL-8, CCL20, E-selectin) that many stimuli drive.
  - The audit question is whether we have a disease rationale, and on our data we do not.
- **"sST2 is the #2 prognostic protein. Isn't that the IL-33 axis lighting up?"**
  - sST2 is an endogenous IL-33 *decoy* **[background]**. A high value is equally consistent with IL-33 being mopped up.
  - In our data the other IL-1-family receptors and antagonists carry the same prognostic signal (IL-1R2 OR 4.5, IL-18R1 OR 6.1, IL-1Ra OR 3.2; module p = 0.001), alongside the dominant neutrophil module. This is an IL-1-family/neutrophil inflammatory axis, not an IL-33-specific signal. (Only summary statistics were delivered, so co-variation across patients cannot be tested.)
  - sST2 is useful as a stratification marker (§4.4), not as proof of mechanism.
- **"Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. IL-33 wasn't measured."** Agreed, which is why the recommendation includes a short recovery of exactly that measurement. We should not commit further capital before seeing it.
- **"Lower IL-33 staining in inflamed lungs could mean IL-33 was released, so the pathway is active."**
  - Possible **[background: IL-33 is a stored nuclear alarmin]**. It is equally consistent with endothelial down-regulation or cell loss, and IHC cannot distinguish these.
  - Either way, the change follows pneumonia and neutrophils, not SARS-CoV-2, which undermines a *viral* indication specifically.
- **"Some IL-33-inducible proteins (IL-6, IL-8, PTX3, OPG, CCL20) are prognostic."**
  - True for individual proteins, and they become our proposed PD panel (§4.4).
  - As a set, however, the programme is no more prognostic than random proteins.
  - IL-6 has many cellular sources, and its axis is already targeted by standard of care **[background]**.

## 3. What our data show, dataset by dataset

### 3.1 In vitro transcriptomics (endothelial and epithelial cells)

**Design, from the annex.** The relevant arms by cell type:

- **HUVEC** (umbilical vein, single donor, passage 2): vehicle, isotype (IC119), AX-1177 (Compound_47), IL-33red, IL-33red + IC119, IL-33red + AX-1177.
- **HGMEC** (glomerular microvascular, single donor, passage 5): the same arms minus vehicle.
- **A549 and MCF7:** PBS, DMSO, IC119 and AX-1177 only. These lines were never exposed to IL-33.

Timepoints were 6 h and 24 h, with n = 3 per arm, giving 114 libraries. One limma-voom model was fitted across all four cell types. The workbooks contain 26 contrasts covering 26,210 genes.

**Finding 1. IL-33red triggers a large, early NF-κB/inflammatory programme in HUVEC.** (Fig 1; T1)

- **Leading 6-h inductions (log2):** SELE +11.2, CSF2 +10.3, CXCL6 +9.4, CCL20 +9.2, VCAM1 +8.7, CX3CL1 +8.4, CSF3 +8.3, CXCL8 +8.2, CXCL1 +8.2, TNF +7.9, ICAM1 +6.0, IL6 +5.8, F3 (tissue factor) +5.6, IL1A +4.6, IL1B +3.6.
- **Procoagulant shift:** F3 up, SERPINE1 +1.7, THBD −1.0.
- **Barrier and junction genes unchanged:** CDH5, CLDN5, TJP1.
- **By 24 h the 6-h programme has fallen to ~40 % of its 6-h size** (sum ratio 0.41). Late genes include complement (C3 +6.1, CFB, C1S) and an interferon-like set (CXCL10, GBP5, MX2).
- **Counts:** 397 robust responders at 6 h (valid SE, |z| ≥ 5, |log2FC| ≥ 1); 130 at 24 h.

**Finding 2. AX-1177 neutralises this programme essentially completely at 6 h and mostly at 24 h.**

- Plotted against the IL-33 effect, the (IL-33 + AX-1177) vs (IL-33 + isotype) contrast falls on the y = −x line (Fig 1).
- **Conservative estimate** (genes selected on the IL-33 contrast, which biases towards under-estimation): 94 % reversed at 6 h, 76 % at 24 h.
- **Symmetric estimate** (selection on the average of two independent estimates): 99–101 % at 6 h, 90–97 % at 24 h.
- **No non-specific suppression.** The derived (IL-33 + AX-1177) vs vehicle contrast sits at baseline (median −0.004 log2 at 6 h), and AX-1177 alone has no effect on programme genes.

![Figure 1](figures/fig1_huvec_neutralisation.png)

**Finding 4. Glomerular microvascular endothelium responds less and for less time.** (Fig 3)

- The AX-1177-neutralisable IL-33 effect in HGMEC is **57 %** of HUVEC's at 6 h (r = 0.77; 69 % of HUVEC responders FDR-significant in HGMEC). At 24 h it is **33 %** (r = 0.53; 15 % significant).
- Within each cell type, the 24-h neutralisable signal is **18 %** of its 6-h size in HGMEC and **39 %** in HUVEC.

![Figure 3](figures/fig3_hgmec_vs_huvec.png)

**Finding 5. AX-1177 alone is transcriptionally inert.**

- The 20–73 "DE genes" per antibody-alone contrast are artefacts of two kinds:
  - low-count lncRNAs and pseudogenes (AveExpr < 0) with ±3 log2 noise;
  - highly expressed genes with trivial effects and implausibly small SEs (e.g. DCBLD2, log2FC 0.09 with t = 50.5).
- They do not form a consistent set. Only four genes recur across cell types, all low-count transcripts or trivial effects in opposite directions (e.g. HSPA8 −0.19 in HUVEC, +0.16 in HGMEC).
- Seven touch the 410-gene IL-33 programme. Most of these are trivial (|log2FC| < 0.2) or low-count changes. Across the programme as a whole, the median effect of AX-1177 alone is −0.02 to +0.07 log2.
- In A549 and MCF7 this is the only question the design can answer, because neither line was given IL-33.

**Finding 3. The delivered p-values are invalid for 230 genes, including the canonical IL-33 targets.** (Fig 2; T2)

- I checked every gene's reported SE against an empirical null: the difference between (IL-33 + isotype) and (IL-33 alone), which should be zero if the isotype is inert. This can be computed exactly from delivered contrasts and gives four values per gene.
- **For ~26,000 genes the reported SEs are well calibrated** (e.g. reported 0.09 vs observed 0.11).
- **For 230 genes they are inflated 10- to >8,000-fold.** Examples: SELE ×18, VCAM1 ×16, CXCL8 ×13, ICAM1 ×10, CCL2 ×49, PTX3 ×158, IL1RL1 ×100, CXCL1 ×5,100, CDH5 ×8,650.
- The list is dominated by genes whose expression differs sharply between the endothelial and epithelial lines (CDH5, PECAM1, VWF, TEK, ERG vs KRT8/19, GATA3). That fits the annex's single model pooled across four unrelated cell types.

![Figure 2](figures/fig2_se_diagnostics.png)

### 3.2 Serum proteomics (100 hospitalised COVID-19 patients, 44 with ICU admission, invasive ventilation or death)

**Design, from the annex.** Serum was taken at enrolment and run on 13 Olink Target-96 panels. Each assay was tested in a logistic model adjusted for age, sex and NEWS2.

**Finding 6. A broad, one-directional severity signature.**

- **Volume:** 178 assays (169 unique proteins) are significant at FDR < 5 %.
- **Direction:** 376 of 384 nominal hits have OR > 1.
- **Leading proteins:** HGF (OR 4.99 per log unit, q = 0.004), sST2 (3.02, q = 0.007), HDGF, SEMA4C, PTX3 (11.6, q = 0.012), ANXA1, CLEC5A, IL-6 (2.14, q = 0.012).
- **Composition:** many hits are intracellular proteins (NPM1, PXN, CALR, PRDX1/6, GSTP1). In clotted serum these probably reflect cell lysis or ex-vivo release **[background]**.
- **Reproducibility:** 27 proteins measured on 2–4 panels give concordant ORs (IL-6 ×4: 2.07–2.31; HGF ×2: 4.99/4.84; OPG ×2: 6.77/4.40). This is a useful internal replication.

**Finding 7. The signature is neutrophil/IL-1-family/IL-6 biology, not endothelial or type-2.** (Fig 4; T7)

**Finding 8. The in-vitro IL-33 programme is not over-represented among prognostic proteins.**

- Of 60 serum proteins encoded by core-programme genes, 13 (22 %) are FDR-significant vs 144/843 (17 %) for the rest. Fisher OR 1.34, p = 0.23.
- A looser programme definition gives the same result (83 proteins; 19 % vs 17 %; p = 0.38).
- The magnitude of in-vitro induction is unrelated to prognostic strength (ρ = 0.009).
- **Individually prognostic IL-33 outputs (FDR < 5 %):** IL-6, PTX3, OPG, IL-18R1, CCL20, IL-8, IL-1β and CSF-1, plus five intracellular or other proteins (B4GALT1, TRIM21, BIRC2, SERPINB8, CTSS). Near the threshold: MCP-1 (q = 0.051), tissue factor (q = 0.051), E-selectin (q = 0.091).

### 3.3 Post-mortem lung immunohistochemistry (31 donors, 74 blocks)

**How it was analysed.** All inference is per donor. Blocks from one donor are not independent (ICC 0.44–0.63), and three COVID+ donors contribute 8 blocks each.

**Group labels vs pathology.** The delivered labels often contradict the pathology text:

- 4 of the 10 "COVID-negative pneumonia" donors have no pneumonia recorded;
- 3 of the 5 "non-pneumonia" controls have acute lung infection or inflammation (coccidioidomycosis, acute pneumonitis, acute and organising pneumonia);
- there are no healthy-lung controls: every COVID-negative donor has significant lung or systemic disease.

I therefore analysed both the nominal groups and pathology-verified groups.

**Finding 9. No IL-33 up-regulation in COVID-19. Vascular IL-33 is lower where there is acute neutrophilic inflammation.** (Fig 5)

- **Epithelial IL-33:** no consistent association with COVID, pneumonia or inflammation (significant in only one of three labelling schemes).
- **Endothelial IL-33:**
  - near the top of the scale in controls (median 5.5 on a stated 0–5 scale; see §6);
  - lower in COVID+ donors (p = 0.052);
  - more clearly lower with acute pneumonia of any cause (age-adjusted β = −0.37, p = 0.007);
  - inversely related to neutrophil elastase (ρ = −0.43, p = 0.016);
  - rises strongly with age (ρ = 0.55, p = 0.001).
- **Robustness:** across three labelling schemes the pneumonia effect has the same direction (β −0.37 to −0.46) but its p-value ranges from 0.007 to 0.12. The COVID term is never significant once pneumonia is included. **This is fragile, hypothesis-level evidence.**
- **Block-level testing would mislead:** it gives p = 0.017 (endothelium) and 0.007 (smooth muscle) for COVID+ vs COVID−. That is false precision from pseudo-replication.
- **The correct way to use every block:** a mixed model with a donor random effect agrees with the donor-level analysis (endothelium β = −0.43, p = 0.048; smooth muscle β = −0.72, p = 0.042). This is a modest *decrease*, not an increase. All tissue statistics are in T8.

**Finding 10. Weak eosinophil and type-2 signals.**

- Eosinophil markers (EPX, EDN, MBP) are low overall (donor medians ≤ 1.5 on 0–5).
- They do not differ between COVID+ and COVID− pneumonia.
- EDN and MBP are higher in COVID+ than in the five heterogeneous "non-pneumonia" controls only (p ≈ 0.013).

### 3.4 Synthesis: the bridge that is not there

For the viral-indication thesis to hold, three links must connect:

1. IL-33 is released or elevated in the diseased lung.
2. IL-33 drives an endothelial programme.
3. That programme's outputs track clinical deterioration.

**Link 2 is strong** (in vitro). **Link 1 is unsupported**: the tissue shows no increase, and the serum IL-33 result was not delivered. **Link 3 fails as a set**, although individual outputs (IL-6, IL-8, CCL20, PTX3, OPG, TF, E-selectin) are prognostic; these become the bridging PD markers in §4.4.

## 4. Forward view

### 4.1 What we would be dropping, and what we keep

- **Dropping:**
  - the severe lower respiratory viral infection programme;
  - a molecule with clean, specific IL-33red-neutralising endothelial pharmacology that is untested against oxidised IL-33, for potency, or in lung cells.
- **Keeping, at no cost:**
  - the AX-1177-reversible endothelial IL-33 signature (T1b; 410 genes);
  - a cleaned, protein-level serum prognostic map for hospitalised COVID-19 (sST2, HGF, IL-6, neutrophil activation).

  Both are reusable by other respiratory assets.

### 4.3 What the next studies would have to establish

1. **Target presence and activity in patients.** Is IL-33 (free; reduced vs oxidised) detectable in serum or BAL of patients who deteriorate? Is it associated with outcome *independently* of NEWS2, sST2 and IL-6? This is the link that is missing today.
2. **Human relevance of the mechanism.** Does material from patients (serum or BAL) activate primary *lung microvascular* endothelium in an AX-1177-sensitive way? What fraction of that activation is IL-33-dependent?
3. **Pharmacology fit for a clinical dose.** Needed: potency (IC50/IC90) against reduced *and* oxidised IL-33, with and without pre-incubation; multi-donor lung endothelium and airway epithelium; a 1–24 h time course.
4. **Specificity of the pathogen signal.** Does the tissue or serum IL-33 signal differ between viral and non-viral pneumonia? The answer decides between a viral and a pathogen-agnostic indication.
5. **Measurable target engagement and PD.** A drug-tolerant free/total IL-33 assay, plus a PD panel that moves in patient-derived systems.

### 4.4 Biomarkers: roles in patients and sampling timepoint

**Sampling timepoint**

- **Patients, primary: T0 = enrolment or hospital admission, before the first dose.**
  - This is the only timepoint at which our outcome associations were measured.
  - In vitro, the IL-33-driven, AX-1177-reversible signal is front-loaded: by 24 h it has fallen to 39 % of its 6-h size in HUVEC and 18 % in HGMEC. Dosing and biomarker reads therefore both belong early in admission.
- **On-treatment PD read: 6 h after the first dose, repeated at 24 h.**
  - 6 h is where drug-vs-isotype separation is maximal in vitro, and it is practical in hospitalised patients.
  - 24 h is the latest point at which our data still show separation (76–97 % of the remaining IL-33 effect reversed in HUVEC).
- **In vitro: 6 h is the primary readout** (maximal induction and drug separation), with 24 h secondary. 24 h should not be the primary endpoint, because only 18 % of the HGMEC signal remains.

### 4.5 Stage-gated plan with explicit decision criteria

**Stage 0: recover what we already own (≤ 3 months; analysis only; near-zero cost).**

- **0a. Serum.** Obtain all 1,194 Olink results, IL-33 first (also TSLP, IL-4, IL-5, IL-13), with the % above LOD by outcome group. Re-fit with effects per SD and collapse duplicate assays. Test IL-33 adjusted for NEWS2, sST2 and IL-6.
- **0b. Tissue.** Retrieve the lung tissue RNA recorded against 22 blocks from 22 donors (IDs Zr4940_*). Score the 6-h core IL-33 programme (T1b) by pneumonia and COVID status. Get a second, blinded IL-33 IHC read on raw 0–5 scores, and pathologist re-adjudication of the groups.
- **0c. RNA-seq.** Refit the endothelial lines separately from the archived counts, and extract the missing contrasts (IL-33 + isotype vs isotype; IL-33 + AX-1177 vs AX-1177; HGMEC). Expected to confirm §3.1; not a decision driver.

**Gate 0 (re-entry), to be written down before anyone looks at the retrieved results.**

- **Primary (needed to re-enter):** circulating IL-33 is detectable in a meaningful share of progressors (e.g. ≥ 25 % above LOD). It is also associated with the composite endpoint independently of NEWS2, sST2 and IL-6 (e.g. OR ≥ 1.5 per SD, p < 0.05).
- **Supportive (cannot re-open the asset on its own):** the core IL-33 programme score is higher in acutely inflamed lung tissue.
- **If the primary criterion is not met: terminate or out-license.** A negative serum result would not prove IL-33 irrelevant, because it can act locally **[background]**. It would, however, leave no measurable human IL-33 signal for enrichment or target engagement, and a development programme needs both.

**Stage 1: test human relevance (~6 months; only if Gate 0 passes).**

- Apply stored serum from the same cohort (kept at −80 °C per the annex), and BAL if available, from progressors vs non-progressors to primary lung microvascular endothelium (≥ 3 donors), ± AX-1177 vs isotype, with a 6-h readout.
- Complete the pharmacology: dose–response against reduced and oxidised IL-33, pre-incubation, airway epithelium (ALI).
- Build the free/total IL-33 assay.
- **Gate 1:** a pre-specified AX-1177-sensitive fraction of patient-sample-induced activation (e.g. ≥ 30 %), and potency compatible with a clinical dose.

**Stage 2: clinical PoC (only if Gate 1 passes).**

- **Population:** severe pneumonia, enriched for high baseline sST2. Pathogen-agnostic unless Stages 0–1 show viral specificity.
- **Primary endpoints:** target engagement and PD at T0 (pre-dose), 6 h and 24 h.
- **Clinical endpoints:** exploratory.

## 5. Considered and ruled out

### 5.1 Indications

Severe viral LRTI as framed, COPD, asthma/type-2, kidney disease and thrombo-inflammation as a standalone indication are all assessed in §4.2. Two further lines of reasoning were considered and set aside:

- **Kidney, inferred from the choice of HGMEC.** The cell line was a comparator, not evidence of renal disease relevance.
- **Keeping the asset on the strength of anti-IL-33 being pursued elsewhere.** That is external to our data, and in any case concerns epithelial/oxidised-IL-33 biology, which we never tested.

### 5.2 Interpretations and analyses examined and rejected

1. **"IL-33 does not induce E-selectin, VCAM-1, IL-8 or MCP-1."** Rejected: the delivered SEs for these genes are inflated 10–50× (Fig 2); their log2FCs (+4 to +11) are consistent across three independent arms.
2. **"AX-1177 has off-target activity (20–73 DE genes when given alone)."** Rejected: the hits are artefacts (low-count noise or implausibly small SEs). They rarely recur across cell types (four genes, low-count or opposite-direction) and leave the IL-33 programme untouched (median effect ≈ 0).
3. **"The IL-33 response is endotoxin or contaminant."** Rejected: it is abolished by a specific anti-IL-33 mAb but not by the isotype control.
4. **"AX-1177 is a non-specific anti-inflammatory."** Rejected: the IL-33 + AX-1177 residual sits at vehicle level, and AX-1177 alone does nothing.
5. **"IL-33 is elevated in COVID-19 lungs."** Rejected: no compartment is higher. The apparent block-level significance is pseudo-replication, and it points the other way.
6. **"sST2 at #2 validates IL-33 as the driver."** Rejected as proof: it is a decoy, and the whole IL-1-family module is prognostic in the same way. Retained as a stratification marker.
7. **"Rank serum proteins by OR."** Rejected: ORs per log unit depend on assay dynamic range (SPON2 OR 93, CSF-1 OR 90), so I ranked by Wald z.
8. **"178 significant serum assays."** Corrected to 169 proteins, because 27 proteins sit on several panels.
9. **"HGMEC responds less because AX-1177 neutralises less well there."** Not ruled out, and cannot be tested with the delivered contrasts (no vehicle arm). Flagged for Stage 0c.
10. **"Lower tissue IL-33 means less IL-33 activity."** Not concluded: IHC cannot separate release from down-regulation.
11. **"Eosinophil-driven COVID pathology."** Rejected: eosinophil scores are low and not different from other pneumonias.
12. **"A549/MCF7 tell us about epithelial IL-33 biology."** Rejected: neither line received IL-33.
13. **"Use 24 h as the primary in-vitro timepoint."** Rejected: by 24 h the reversible signal is 39 % (HUVEC) and 18 % (HGMEC) of its 6-h size.

## 6. Data-integrity and analysis issues the committee should know about (full log: T6)

**RNA-seq**

- **Pooled model gives invalid SEs.** One model pooled across four unrelated cell types yields invalid SEs for 230 genes, including the canonical IL-33 targets. AveExpr is averaged over all 114 libraries and is uninformative for any single cell type.
- **Workbook structure.** The sheets named "6h" and "24h" do not hold timepoints: both contain the same 24 contrasts, one padded with 419,535 blank rows.
- **Labelling.** The HUVEC comparator is labelled both "Vehicle" and "Nontreated".
- **Design gaps.** There is no HGMEC vehicle arm, and the epithelial lines were never given IL-33. Everything else rests on a single donor, a single dose, concurrent addition and reduced IL-33 only. DMSO is listed as a vehicle for an antibody, which is not plausible.

**Serum**

- **Undelivered analyses.** 100 of 1,194 analysed assays were not delivered, including IL-33. By reconstruction, about 16 of them had p < 0.05.
- **Duplicate assays.** Proteins measured on several panels were treated as independent assays.
- **Incomparable ORs.** ORs per log unit cannot be compared across assays, and it is unclear whether NPX (already log2) was log-transformed again.
- **Undefined columns.** Two columns (`composite_endpoint_0/1`, values k/56 and k/44) are not defined in the annex.
- **Missing adjustments.** There is no adjustment for renal function, time from symptom onset or treatment.

**Post-mortem**

- **Pseudo-replication.** 74 blocks come from only 31 donors.
- **Scale problems.**
  - Derived score columns are raw score + 0.5 for non-zero values (0 stays 0).
  - The IL-33 scores exist only in this derived form. They reach 5.5 on a stated 0–5 scale and include quarter-point values and a live formula (`=(2+4.5)/2`).
- **Labels contradict pathology** (§3.3).
- **Missing and limited fields.** One donor has no recorded SARS-CoV-2 status; scoring was by a single reader; donors are aged 64–98 and all white.
- **Undelivered resource.** Tissue RNA exists for 22 blocks (22 donors) but was not delivered.

**Limits of this review**

- **No count matrices or patient-level data:**
  - RNA-seq SE validity was assessed empirically;
  - serum analyses work from summary statistics;
  - tissue groups were re-adjudicated by me from the pathology text and need pathologist confirmation.
- **Olink name mapping:** Olink assay names were mapped to genes by hand (mapping in T3 and `scripts/olink_map.py`).
- **Inflammation panel content:** taken from the vendor's panel list and should be verified.
